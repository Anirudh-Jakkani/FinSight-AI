Disclaimer

FinSight AI is an educational personal-finance analytics project.

Not financial advice. Nothing in this application — summaries, categorizations, forecasts, anomaly flags, or AI-generated text — constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Always consult a licensed professional before making financial decisions.

Educational purpose

FinSight AI was built as a learning and portfolio project to demonstrate a full-stack personal-finance analytics pipeline: transaction ingestion, rule-based and machine-learning categorization, spending analytics, recurring-payment and anomaly detection, forecasting, and an AI assistant grounded in that data. It is not a commercial product and is not audited, certified, or regulated as a financial service.

Sample data

The demo dataset bundled with this project (data/sample_transactions.csv) is entirely synthetic, generated by a seeded script. It does not describe any real person's finances.

Accuracy of automated analysis

Category labels, recurring-payment detection, anomaly flags, and expense forecasts are produced by simple, explainable rules and statistical models over whatever transaction data has been entered. They can be wrong, incomplete, or based on too little history to be reliable — this is disclosed directly in the relevant API responses (for example, a forecast method of insufficient_data or naive_last_month).

AI-generated content

The "Ask FinSight" summary and chat features send your verified, already-computed figures to a third-party large language model and ask it to narrate them in plain language. The application does not let the model invent numbers, but it can still misstate a comparison, omit context, or phrase something misleadingly. Treat AI responses as a starting point for your own review, not a final answer.
