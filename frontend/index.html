Features

Everything from raw CSV to clear answers

⟳

One-click pipeline

Upload a CSV and one call ingests, deduplicates, cleans merchant names, categorizes, and applies ML fallbacks — end to end.

◨

Smart categorization

Deterministic keyword rules handle the obvious cases with high confidence; a TF-IDF classifier steps in only where the rules genuinely aren't sure.

📈

Analytics & forecasting

Income, expenses, savings rate, monthly trends, and a transparent linear-trend expense forecast — every number traceable back to your transactions.

⚠

Recurring & anomaly detection

Automatically finds your real monthly bills and subscriptions, and flags transactions that are statistical outliers for their category.

✦

Ask FinSight

Chat with an AI analyst about your own data. It's instructed to use only the verified figures it's handed — never to invent a number.

Private by account

Every request is tied to your signed-in session — your transactions, chats, and analytics are never visible to any other account.
